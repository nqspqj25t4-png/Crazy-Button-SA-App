Privacy

Analytics and cookies · crazybuttonsa.web.app

What we measure

Only if you tap Accept, we measure how the site is used so we can make the drops better:

  • Pages and products viewed, and how you found us (for example Instagram or Google).
  • What goes into the bag, what comes out, and whether an order is started.
  • Where people click and scroll, using click heatmaps and recorded sessions with form typing hidden.

Your device type, browser and approximate country or city come along with this. We do not collect your name, email or phone number through analytics.

Who helps us

Google Analytics (Google) and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft) process this data for us. Advertising features are switched off, and we never sell your data.

Your choice

If you tap Decline, none of these tools load. You can change your mind at any time.

Contact

Questions or a request to access or delete your data: message us on Instagram @crazybuttonsa. This notice follows South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
