04 — The calendar

Six weeks.
Six drops.

A new drop every week for six weeks, each built for a different crew. Limited runs, no re-ups.

Week 01

No Re-Up

Because once it’s gone, it’s gone. Small runs, numbered pieces and a 24-hour cop window. For sneakerheads, resale kings and street flexers.

Limited drop
Week 02

Raw Threads

No filter, no polish, just steeze. Heavy denim, beatable tees and jackets that get better with every scuff.

Core drop
Week 03

Lowkey Legend

Speak in style, not volume. Tonal, curated and quietly loud. For alt fashion, designers and underground creators.

Aesthetic drop
Week 04

Tunnel Flex

Built for tunnel walks. Off-court fits for on-point energy: clean lines, stretch and dry-tech.

Functional drop
Week 05

Choose Your Fit

You vote. We stitch. The community picks the next hoodie colour. The winning colourway drops to voters first.

Community vote
Week 06

Collab Week

Made with the culture, for the culture. A creator-designed cap or tee with young SA artists, dancers and gqom and amapiano producers.
