RouteOne

Six weeks, six drops, one route through the city. A new drop every week, each built for a different crew. Limited runs, no re-ups.

  1. No Re-Up

    Because once it’s gone, it’s gone. Small runs, numbered pieces and a 24-hour cop window.

    Week one · Limited drop

  2. Raw Threads

    No filter, no polish, just steeze. Heavy denim, beatable tees, jackets that get better with every scuff.

    Week two · Core drop

  3. Lowkey Legend

    Speak in style, not volume. Tonal, curated and quietly loud, for alt fashion and underground creators.

    Week three · Aesthetic drop

  4. Tunnel Flex

    Built for tunnel walks. Off-court fits with clean lines, stretch and dry-tech.

    Week four · Functional drop

  5. Choose Your Fit

    You vote, we stitch. The community picks the next hoodie colour and voters get it first.

    Week five · Community vote

  6. Collab Week

    Made with the culture: a cap or tee designed with young SA artists, dancers and gqom and amapiano producers.

    Week six · Micro collab

    Questions
    from the block

    How do I order from Crazy Button SA?
    Add pieces to your bag in the online shop and send the order on WhatsApp. We confirm sizes, delivery and payment with you there. Card payment on the site is coming.
    Where is Crazy Button SA from?
    Crazy Button SA is a streetwear brand from KwaMashu, Umlazi and Durban in South Africa, started in 2011.
    What sizes do you make?
    Hoodies, jackets, knits, shirts, tees and track pants come in XS to XXL. Bucket hats, bags and patches are one size, and luggage covers come in S, M and L.
    How much are the patches?
    Each iron-on patch from Patch Drop Vol. 1 is R349, and the full pack of all twelve is R2,799.
    How often do new drops land?
    Route One runs six drops over six weeks, one a week, each in a limited run with no re-ups.
