Born on two streets

Crazy Button started in KwaMashu and Umlazi with a needle, a thread and a point to prove. Four holes, flag colours, no shortcuts. Fifteen years later it carries Durban to the world.

RouteOne

Six weeks, six drops, one route through the city. A new drop every week, each built for a different crew. Limited runs, no re-ups.

  1. No Re-Up

    Because once it’s gone, it’s gone. Small runs, numbered pieces and a 24-hour cop window.

    Week one · Limited drop

  2. Raw Threads

    No filter, no polish, just steeze. Heavy denim, beatable tees, jackets that get better with every scuff.

    Week two · Core drop

  3. Lowkey Legend

    Speak in style, not volume. Tonal, curated and quietly loud, for alt fashion and underground creators.

    Week three · Aesthetic drop

  4. Tunnel Flex

    Built for tunnel walks. Off-court fits with clean lines, stretch and dry-tech.

    Week four · Functional drop

  5. Choose Your Fit

    You vote, we stitch. The community picks the next hoodie colour and voters get it first.

    Week five · Community vote

  6. Collab Week

    Made with the culture: a cap or tee designed with young SA artists, dancers and gqom and amapiano producers.

    Week six · Micro collab
